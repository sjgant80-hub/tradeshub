The trades kernel

Grounded in your rates, your patch, your specialities.

The kernel is what stops the AI from quoting outside your service radius, promising specialties you don't offer, or undercutting your rates. It encodes: your postcode centre + radius, your specialities, your not-offered list, your callout rate, hourly rate, emergency multiplier, insurance certs, chase cadence, invoice terms.

The AI can't quote commercial kitchens if the kernel says not_offered: ["commercial_kitchens"]. Can't schedule callouts outside your radius. Can't reply to landlord regulars in the same tone as one-off homeowners — because it reads the kernel first.

Your data stays local. Every kernel field lives in your browser or your own repo. Zero data ever hits AI-Native Solutions servers.
